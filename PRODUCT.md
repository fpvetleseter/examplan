# Product

## Register

product

## Users
IE law and international relations students juggling several midterms or finals, unfinished readings and often a part-time job. They open ExamPlan on a phone between classes or on a laptop in the evening, wanting to know what to study today and whether they can realistically finish before the exam. They will close anything that asks for uploads, onboarding questions or an AI key.

## Product Purpose
Turn an exam's date, available daily time and topics into a manageable day-by-day revision sequence, so a student can start a useful session instead of planning for an hour. Success: saving an exam takes seconds, today's session is visible without hunting, and plans stay honest about what they don't know (no invented cases, readings or professor expectations).

## Brand Personality
Composed, scholarly, reassuring. The voice is plain and calm, never hype or fake urgency. It should feel like a quiet study refuge with academic gravitas, not a productivity hustle app or an AI chat.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI chat or "AI study buddy" interfaces.
- SaaS dashboards with hero metrics, fake stats, testimonials or user counts.
- Gamified streak apps, confetti, mascots, emoji icons.
- Stock illustrations, gradient-on-everything, neon or purple-blue "AI" palettes.
- Anything implying university endorsement or professor-specific content.

## Design Principles
1. Next action first: the student sees what to do today, the exam date and the workload without hunting.
2. Honest by construction: copy and UI only claim what the app actually does; generic plans are labelled generic.
3. Calm under pressure: exam stress is the context, so the interface lowers the temperature rather than adding urgency.
4. Phone between classes: every flow works one-handed at 375px with no sideways scroll.
5. Never lose work: saving is independent of planning, and failures keep the student's input.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text and UI boundaries), including text over the background painting. Visible focus rings, keyboard operation, labelled fields, associated errors, aria-live success messages, touch targets at least 44px. Respect prefers-reduced-motion; motion limited to short opacity/transform transitions. Errors stay a clearly readable red, never colour-only.
